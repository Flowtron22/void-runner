// HOW TO PLAY

BUILD. DEPLOY. ROLL.

  1. Choose a faction.Each faction loads points, Keywords, Leadership, Objective Control, weapons and abilities from the KS-derived roster.
  2. Build and attach.Stay under the point limit. To join a Leader, select it and tap an eligible deployed Bodyguard unit.
  3. Watch the conditions.Weapon rules activate only when their condition is shown. Round 1 is Long Range; round 2 onward is Half Range.
  4. Follow command status.CP powers automatic core Stratagems. Below-half-strength units test Battle-shock and cannot receive Stratagems when shocked.
  5. Improve the campaign.Choose a temporary Battle Honour and equip recovered Wargear before the next mission.
