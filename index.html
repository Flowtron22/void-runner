// HOW TO PLAY

BUILD. DEPLOY. ROLL.

  1. Choose the campaign.Higher point limits add sectors, longer routes, stronger enemies and larger permanent rewards.
  2. Build and attach.Stay under the point limit. Select an unlocked unit, then tap the deployment grid.
  3. Claim stars.Every area offers three threats. More stars mean more danger and unlock stronger final bosses.
  4. Let the dice resolve.Combat is automatic. Hit, wound, save and damage rolls remain visible as they resolve.
  5. Choose one reward.Post-War Affairs offers Requisition, an Honour or a Stratagem. Some areas reveal recruits or stores.
